# Surface: portfolio home page (src/App.jsx)

Mode: Experience (portfolio); the recruiter's action is "Email me".
Audience/job/proof: see PRODUCT.md. Proof = 12 lab PDFs, each mapped to the device it was built on; 3 certs with verify links.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The portfolio is Yuuki's home lab, labelled. Every lab is filed on the device it configured, and you open it by picking that device. It refuses the category default: name, tagline, a projects grid of cards, a skills list.
OWN-WORLD:
- The ground is evening indigo #12172A, with lamp amber #FFB547 as the single accent, and an off-white label-maker tape that carries every name.
- Mono numbers are tabular and there are no rounded corners.
- The world is recognisable with the content removed: tape strips, amber "lit" state, indigo field.
STORY: A recruiter sees who Yuuki is and what he certifies. They watch the packet run through his real lab gear, pick a device, open a lab PDF, and email him.
FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Desktop 1280: the plate fills the hero band full-bleed. A left text column, about 30%, sits over the plate's darker left edge (bed and shelves) and holds the name (h1), the title, one sentence and the amber "Email me" button.
- Over the plate: tape labels on six devices, an amber pulse running the cable path, and a stepper row of six tape buttons along the bottom edge.
- Phone 390: text first, then a 4:5 plate window where the camera centres the active device.
FORM: Data-Centre Wayfinding (assigned roll #5, seed 47d7e8fa), translated into the user-chosen home-lab plate. Raises kept: a running head naming the current span, lit = you are here, fixed tabular numbers, the plate never boxed.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
